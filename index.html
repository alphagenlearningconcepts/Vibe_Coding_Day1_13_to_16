PROMPTING PRINCIPLES

Have you prompted before?

Understanding what prompting is and why AI is a support tool, not a dependency:

What is Prompting?

Prompting is giving structured, precise instructions to an AI model to guide its generation toward a specific coding, design, or logical output.

Why AI must be held as a SUPPORT, not a DEPENDENCY:

  • • Energy & Compute Consumption: Over-prompting or relying blindly on AI consumes excessive server energy and computation.
  • • Critical Thinking & Growth: Total dependency on technology reduces your own problem-solving capabilities.
  • • Tool Mindset: We use AI as a powerful assistant while keeping human intelligence in command of architecture and decisions.

PROMPT STRUCTURE

Let's Explore Prompting 🎯

Here are the core ingredients required to compose an effective AI prompt:

Key Prompt Ingredients:

  • ✅ Persona / Role: e.g. "Act as a Senior Web Developer"
  • ✅ Project Goal & Task: e.g. "Build a Space Dodge Game"
  • ✅ Tech Stack: e.g. "Single file HTML5 Canvas + JavaScript"
  • ✅ UI & Design Rules: e.g. "Dark space theme, score counter, starfield background"
  • ✅ Constraints: e.g. "Include arrow key controls, restart button, responsive layout"
# Game Prompt Example:

"Act as an expert game developer. Create a responsive Space Dodger game in HTML/CSS/JavaScript contained in one HTML file. Include player spaceship moved by Arrow keys, falling asteroids to dodge, score multiplier over time, collision detection, and restart screen."
